# Generated UI art

`JournalBackground.png` and `WoodJournal.png` were generated with the built-in
OpenAI ImageGen tool on 2026-09-24 for Topaz. No third-party asset pack was
imported for these files. The journal was derived from the inventory concept
of a leather-bound field journal with blank parchment and muted-sepia landscape details; Wood was
generated as a separate transparent illustration. The journal plate removes all labels, items and scenery outside the book; the wood plate depicts tied logs as a transparent ink/watercolor illustration. Temporary concept images are not required production inputs.

All live labels, values, focus states, and interactions are rendered by Unity
on top of the static plates. The concept world's cabins, coast, and terrain
are visual exploration only and are not included in these production assets.
