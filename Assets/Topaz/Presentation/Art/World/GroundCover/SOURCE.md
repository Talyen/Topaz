# Alpine ground-cover derivatives

`LuminousAlpineSetup.Apply` creates normalized detail meshes from the owned Alpine Mountain grass and flower-bush meshes plus the distinct shared PNB core grass mesh. Low, upright and shade grass are authored growth-form/scale/color variations of those two grass sources, not claimed additional vendor models. Geometry and UVs derive from those restricted packages; generated `.asset` mesh payloads are ignored and must not be publicly redistributed. Metadata, prefab references and owned material settings preserve stable bindings. Restore the owned packages, then run `Topaz/Generation/Configure Luminous Alpine` through Unity to regenerate the local meshes and prepare their native GI dependencies. Vendor source geometry and textures remain unchanged.

`WorldShaderSetup.NativeFoliageLods` also generates the ignored `PNB Native Fade.shadergraph` from the owned PNB Foliage graph. It enables URP native signed LOD crossfading and time-based deformation motion vectors; all vendor material inputs remain available. The private graph payload must not be publicly redistributed. Restore the PNB source and run the authoring command to regenerate it. Repeated grass uses the artist-authored 64-vertex Alpine mesh; the sparse flower detail uses the 552-vertex mesh rather than its 2,992-vertex near-prop mesh.

The derivative specializes the curated non-emissive, unfrosted foliage: emission, pulse, frosting and extra back-face-lighting toggles are fixed off, allowing their unused branches to be removed by shader compilation. Retained leaf/trunk normals, palette inputs, wind and native passes preserve the supported material treatment. A source fingerprint avoids regenerating unchanged graphs during material or LOD authoring.
