# Alpine ground-cover derivatives

`LuminousAlpineSetup.Apply` creates normalized detail meshes from the owned Alpine Mountain grass and flower-bush meshes plus the shared PNB core grass mesh. Those `.asset` payloads derive from restricted geometry/UVs and remain ignored; metadata and wrapper bindings preserve stable identity. The current Low, Upright and Shade Grass wrappers are subsequently rebound by `FineAlpineGrassSetup.Apply` to original procedural meshes under `../FineGrass/`; only their materials/textures retain PNB dependencies. Meadow Flowers still uses the reduced vendor-derived mesh. Restore missing owned packages/derivatives with `Topaz/Generation/Configure Luminous Alpine`; it also prepares native GI dependencies. Vendor source geometry and textures remain unchanged.

`WorldShaderSetup.NativeFoliageLods` generates the ignored `PNB Native Fade.shadergraph` and `PNB Grass Fade.shadergraph` from the owned PNB Foliage graph. Canopy/bush materials use native signed LOD crossfading and time-based deformation motion vectors. Grass uses per-clump distance fade and explicit previous-wind custom motion, without forcing an LODGroup crossfade onto Terrain details. Both incorporate the gameplay scenery cutaway. Private graph payloads must not be publicly redistributed; regenerate them locally from restored PNB sources. The sparse flower detail uses the 552-vertex mesh rather than its 2,992-vertex near-prop mesh.

The derivative specializes the curated non-emissive, unfrosted foliage: emission, pulse, frosting and extra back-face-lighting toggles are fixed off, allowing their unused branches to be removed by shader compilation. Retained leaf/trunk normals, palette inputs, wind and native passes preserve the supported material treatment. A source fingerprint avoids regenerating unchanged graphs during material or LOD authoring.
