# Home crypt first playable slice

Status: implemented for Mac owner review. The first clear is intended to take 20–30 minutes; actual duration and visual readability still need a 60 Hz Mac playtest.

The exterior grave path and crypt entrance now stand at the far side of the [Graveyard](GRAVEYARD_STUDY.md), a walk from the safe Home. The entrance remains open even while outdoor enemies are alive. The crypt loads additively as a separate authored interior while the player, inventory, camera, and save owner remain in Bootstrap. Exiting it returns outside the Graveyard entrance. Six spaces lead through a gallery with one Minion and one Skeleton Rogue, a shielded Warrior hall, a Stone/Iron cache, a midway Campfire and lever, and the Skeleton Mage's shrine. The lever opens a permanent return shortcut. The dungeon has no quest, lore text, or story requirement.

The free KayKit Dungeon, Halloween, Forest, and Skeletons models provide the architecture, grave path, enemies, props, and reward dressing. Only active, unmarked assets are used. Modular Dungeon floors and walls dress simple collider geometry, while near walls stay low and the ceiling remains open for the fixed orthographic camera. The interior dims outdoor sun and ambient light so unshadowed torches can shape the rooms; outdoor rain stays outside. A baked NavMesh follows the collider layout. The first cut is an art and route review, not a performance claim.

The Stone/Iron cache stays visible and can be opened regardless of nearby enemies. It refills 72 in-game hours after a successful claim; insufficient backpack space leaves its reward available. The shortcut lever remains permanent World state.

All crypt Skeletons return 24 in-game hours after defeat, once their spawn is away from the player and out of view. Campfire recovery advances eight hours but does not reset them early. The midway hearth is an enemy safe area so recovery cannot immediately repeat a defeat. The Rogue has six health and alternates between two gallery firing spots. It stands still for a 0.8-second narrow red line warning; aim locks for the last 0.2 seconds. A visible bolt travels at 12 m/s up to 7 m and deals three damage before Armor. The added gallery pillar, a raised correctly faced shield, and dodge can stop the shot. Blocking grants Shield XP but does not stagger the distant Rogue.

The Mage has a short staff sweep and a ground spell. The same `GroundSpellAbility` and `GroundSpellDefinition` drive the Mage and the player's two-handed Crypt Staff: a 0.9-second area warning, 1.35 m radius, faction-colored rim, violet rune, brief particle and light impact, and the same audio clips. Ground damage ignores shields but respects leaving the circle and dodge invulnerability. The player's spell uses Attack, mouse ground position or a 4.5 m right-stick aim point, wall-clamped placement, and a three-second cooldown. A staff cast cannot be interrupted by dodge until recovery.

Each Mage defeat extinguishes the shrine light and leaves one persistent Staff plus one Bone Fragment. The light relights when the Mage returns after its deadline. A full backpack leaves both pickups in the crypt, including after reload. The Staff belongs to the Character who collects it and travels with them. The Staff skill becomes visible on first acquisition. It earns XP for effective spell damage and follows the existing level, talent choice, and active-slot rules. Its talents are Mobile Casting, Far Sigil, Wide Circle, and Dodge Focus. The midway Campfire belongs to each Character–World Visit; defeat advances World time eight hours once and preserves the lever and earned items.

Each Rogue defeat drops one persistent Skeleton Crossbow and one Bone Fragment. The Character who collects a Crossbow owns it across Worlds and can store it. Equipping this two-handed weapon moves the shield to the backpack only when there is room. A deliberate shot tracks mouse aim until release; gamepad aim can favor a visible enemy within eight degrees. A bolt travels at 14 m/s up to 7 m, deals four damage with starter gear, and stops at its first target. The 0.28-second aim cannot be dodged out of; after release the automatic 1.2-second reload slows movement, and a dodge restarts it. The small HUD cue shows reload progress.

Crossbows appears in the Skills journal on acquisition. Effective bolt damage awards its XP; reload becomes 1% quicker per level and damage gains one at levels 5 and 10. Its talent choices are Pierce (20% chance after a damaging first hit to strike one more aligned enemy), Quick Reload (0.18 seconds), Quick Aim (0.07 seconds), and Long Sight (one meter). Collection version 9 replaces the active one-time drop rule with saved enemy deadlines and repeatable pickups. Player and Rogue bolts use the same visible, swept projectile rules; enemy bolts never harm other skeletons.

JaggedStone's original CC0 `magical_1.ogg`, `magical_7.ogg`, and `dungeon_ambient_1.ogg` provide the shared cast, impact, and a quiet interior bed. Kenney CC0 `metalClick.ogg` and `metalLatch.ogg` provide the crossbow shot and ready cues, with its light impact clip on a hit. Exact source and SHA-256 values are in the asset folders' `SOURCE.md` files.
