# Home crypt first playable slice

Status: implemented for Mac owner review. The first clear is intended to take 20–30 minutes; actual duration and visual readability still need a 60 Hz Mac playtest.

The home entrance is a fenced KayKit grave path west of the safe ring. It is open from a fresh World. The crypt loads additively as a separate authored scene while the player, inventory, camera, and save owner remain in Bootstrap. Six spaces lead through a gallery with one Minion and one Skeleton Rogue, a shielded Warrior hall, an optional Stone/Iron cache, a midway Campfire and lever, and the Skeleton Mage's shrine. The lever opens a permanent return shortcut. The player can leave at the entrance or retreat from the final room. The dungeon has no quest, lore text, or story requirement.

The free KayKit Dungeon, Halloween, Forest, and Skeletons models provide the architecture, grave path, enemies, props, and reward dressing. Only active, unmarked assets are used. Modular Dungeon floors and walls dress simple collider geometry, while near walls stay low and the ceiling remains open for the fixed orthographic camera. The interior dims outdoor sun and ambient light so unshadowed torches can shape the rooms; outdoor rain stays outside. A baked NavMesh follows the collider layout. The first cut is an art and route review, not a performance claim.

Ordinary enemies reset on a new visit and on Campfire recovery. The midway hearth is an enemy safe area so recovery cannot immediately repeat a defeat. The Rogue has six health and alternates between two gallery firing spots. It stands still for a 0.8-second narrow red line warning; aim locks for the last 0.2 seconds. A visible bolt travels at 12 m/s up to 7 m and deals three damage before Armor. The added gallery pillar, a raised correctly faced shield, and dodge can stop the shot. Blocking grants Shield XP but does not stagger the distant Rogue.

The Mage has a short staff sweep and a ground spell. The same `GroundSpellAbility` and `GroundSpellDefinition` drive the Mage and the player's two-handed Crypt Staff: a 0.9-second area warning, 1.35 m radius, faction-colored rim, violet rune, brief particle and light impact, and the same audio clips. Ground damage ignores shields but respects leaving the circle and dodge invulnerability. The player's spell uses Attack, mouse ground position or a 4.5 m right-stick aim point, wall-clamped placement, and a three-second cooldown. A staff cast cannot be interrupted by dodge until recovery.

Defeating the Mage extinguishes the shrine light and records one persistent staff pickup. A full backpack leaves it in the crypt, including after reload. The Mage stays defeated for that World; the staff belongs to the Character who collects it and travels with them. The Staff skill becomes visible on first acquisition. It earns XP for effective spell damage and follows the existing level, talent choice, and active-slot rules. Its talents are Mobile Casting, Far Sigil, Wide Circle, and Dodge Focus. The midway Campfire belongs to each Character–World Visit; defeat advances World time eight hours once and preserves the lever, cache, Mage defeat, and earned items.

The first Rogue defeat in a World drops one persistent Skeleton Crossbow pickup; later defeats grant no repeat loot. It remains if the backpack is full. The Character who collects it owns it across Worlds and can store it. Equipping this two-handed weapon moves the shield to the backpack only when there is room. A deliberate shot tracks mouse aim until release; gamepad aim can favor a visible enemy within eight degrees. A bolt travels at 14 m/s up to 7 m, deals four damage with starter gear, and stops at its first target. The 0.28-second aim cannot be dodged out of; after release the automatic 1.2-second reload slows movement, and a dodge restarts it. The small HUD cue shows reload progress.

Crossbows appears in the Skills journal on acquisition. Effective bolt damage awards its XP; reload becomes 1% quicker per level and damage gains one at levels 5 and 10. Its talent choices are Pierce (20% chance after a damaging first hit to strike one more aligned enemy), Quick Reload (0.18 seconds), Quick Aim (0.07 seconds), and Long Sight (one meter). Collection version 8 adds the skill and a World-owned first-drop flag without altering previous rewards. Player and Rogue bolts use the same visible, swept projectile rules; enemy bolts never harm other skeletons.

JaggedStone's original CC0 `magical_1.ogg`, `magical_7.ogg`, and `dungeon_ambient_1.ogg` provide the shared cast, impact, and a quiet interior bed. Kenney CC0 `metalClick.ogg` and `metalLatch.ogg` provide the crossbow shot and ready cues, with its light impact clip on a hit. Exact source and SHA-256 values are in the asset folders' `SOURCE.md` files.
