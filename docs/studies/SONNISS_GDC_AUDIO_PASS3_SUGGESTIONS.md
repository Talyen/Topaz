## What I would stop cutting by filename

Retain core melee and ranged attacks, shields, magic, ordinary creature/animal voices, bone impacts, material handling, gathering/building/cooking, doors/chests, clothing, ground/wood/stone footsteps, simple UI feedback, and natural weather/region ambience. Preserve variations in these useful families.

I would not remove tropical/coastal/snowy ambience solely because those regions are not built yet. Nor would I keep escalating gore cuts without a clearer tone decision: ordinary wet hits and cracks can support combat and monsters. Generic numeric filenames and multiple recordings of the same action need listening before quality-based reduction.

The underwater and contact-recording families were retained in earlier passes because they can support magic and monsters. They appear here only as explicitly optional reductions to speculative coverage. The footprint does not itself make them poor assets.
